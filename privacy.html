1 Overview

X AutoLike & Save Pro is a Firefox browser extension that automatically likes and bookmarks tweets and retweets from selected X (Twitter) accounts.

🔒 Short version: We store your settings locally on your device. We never sell your data. The only external communication is license key validation over HTTPS.

2 Data We Collect

The Extension stores the following data exclusively in your browser's local storage (browser.storage.local). This data stays on your device.

DataPurposeStored Where
X account usernames you addTo know which accounts to monitorLocal only
Daily like countTo enforce free/pro daily limitLocal only
License keyTo validate your Pro subscriptionLocal only
Settings (interval, mode, language)To remember your preferencesLocal only
Scheduler timeTo auto-run at your chosen timeLocal only

3 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ✅ We do not collect your X (Twitter) credentials or passwords
  • ✅ We do not access your X direct messages
  • ✅ We do not store your browsing history
  • ✅ We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • ✅ We do not sell or share any data with third parties
  • ✅ We do not read tweet content beyond what's needed to perform the like/bookmark action

4 License Key Validation

When you enter a Pro license key, the Extension sends only the license key string to our validation server to verify its validity.

Endpoint: xtools-license.alibulutt.workers.dev (Cloudflare Workers)
Method: HTTPS POST — encrypted in transit
Data sent: License key string only — no personal information
Data stored server-side: None — validation is stateless

The key is validated against Lemon Squeezy's license system. No personal data is transmitted or logged during this process.

7 Data Retention

All locally stored data is retained until you:

  • Remove the extension from Firefox
  • Manually clear your browser's local storage
  • Click "Remove license" inside the extension (removes license key only)

We have no ability to access or delete data stored locally on your device.

8 Children's Privacy

This Extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9 Changes to This Policy
